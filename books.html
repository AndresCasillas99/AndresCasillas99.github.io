A short story book I wrote (2020)
In Search of Lost Time
📖 Milestone I am very proud to announce that I have finished reading Marcel Proust's "À la recherche du temps perdu" — all seven volumes! This journey started in 2017 and finished in 2025. It is my absolute favorite book of all time, and I even feel a bit strange without it :(
🏆 My ranking My ranking of its volumes (in Spanish) is:
  1. Volume 4: Sodoma y Gomorra.
  2. Volume 2: A la Sombra de las Muchachas en Flor.
  3. Volume 1: Por el Camino de Swann.
  4. Volume 7: El Tiempo Recobrado.
  5. Volume 5: La Prisionera.
  6. Volume 6: La Fugitiva.
  7. Volume 3: El Mundo de Guermantes.
